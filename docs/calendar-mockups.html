Missing Outlook, Teams, or Google calendars?

iOS Calendar can only see calendars from accounts you've added in iOS Settings. To add them: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → choose your provider. Make sure the Calendar toggle is ON.
